Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Time Collector NoteBook
Developer: Guangzhou Yuanjin Network Technology Co., Ltd.

You can request deletion of your account and associated data for Time Collector NoteBook.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings (or My page) and choose Delete account / Log off.
  3. Confirm the deletion in the dialog. This action cannot be undone.

Alternative: request by email

If you cannot use in-app deletion, email us from the address linked to your account:

admin@gzyuanjintech.cn

Please include:

  • App name: Time Collector NoteBook
  • Your account email / username
  • Subject line: Account deletion request

We will process requests within 14 days.

What data is deleted

  • Account credentials and profile information
  • Notes and content stored under your account on our servers (if any)
  • Purchase / membership status linked to the account (where applicable)

What may be retained

  • Records required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or accounting (for example transaction receipts), retained only as long as required by law
  • Anonymized / aggregated analytics that cannot identify you
